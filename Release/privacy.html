StemSplitter Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The short version:

  • All processing happens on your device. No audio or video is ever uploaded.
  • We collect no data — no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No accounts. The app has no sign-in and no user records of any kind.
  • The app makes no network requests and contains no third-party SDKs.

What StemSplitter does

StemSplitter takes a video from your photo library, extracts its audio, and splits it into vocals and instrumental tracks — entirely on your iPhone, using the device's own processor and Neural Engine. The app works offline (in airplane mode) for videos already stored on your phone.

How your media is handled

  • Video selection: picking a video uses Apple's system photo picker, which runs outside the app. StemSplitter never gains access to your photo library and receives only the one video you chose.
  • Processing: the audio is decoded and split on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any server, because the app has no server and no networking code.
  • Temporary files: split audio files are written only to the app's own caches on your device and are automatically deleted the next time you open the app. They are never uploaded.
  • Exports: when you share or save a WAV file, that file leaves the app only because you send it somewhere (for example to Files or another app). That transfer is handled entirely by iOS at your direction.
  • iCloud videos: if you pick a video that isn't downloaded to your phone, iOS itself fetches it from your own iCloud Photo Library using Apple's system infrastructure. StemSplitter never sends your media anywhere.

Data we collect

None. StemSplitter collects no data of any kind — no identifiers, no usage data, no diagnostics, no location, no contacts, no audio or video content. There are no accounts, so there are no user records to store or breach. The App Store's privacy label for StemSplitter states "Data Not Collected," and the app ships a privacy manifest declaring no tracking, no tracking domains, and no collected data types.

Required-reason API disclosure

To avoid filling your storage, StemSplitter checks how much free disk space is available before starting a split, solely to decide whether there is enough room to write the output files. This falls under Apple's required-reason API category for disk space (reason code E174.1), which is declared in the app's privacy manifest. The app uses no other required-reason APIs and does not read, store, or transmit file timestamps or any other device information.

Children's privacy

Because StemSplitter collects no data at all, it collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of StemSplitter ever collected data or added third-party code, this policy and the App Store privacy label would be updated first. As shipped, the app cannot send your data anywhere.
